What is full form of RAG - Retrieval - Augmented Generation

The Problem RAG solves

Large Language Model(LLMs) like GPT 5.5 , Claude Opus 4.8, Gemini 3.1 and Llama 4, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Kimi K.3, sarvam ai are incredibly powerful at generating human-like text, However, they have 3 critical limitations

**Knowledge Cut Off** : LLMs are trainded on data up to certain date, they do not know about events,documents, or changes that are happened after training

**Hallucination:** When an LLM does not know the answer, it may confidently generate plausible souding but completely incorrect information

**No Access to Private Data:** LLMs Can not access your comapny's internal documents, databases,JIRA tickets, test reports, or properirtary knowledge bases.
